# Adventure-Shop

## Summary

This project is for a fictious retailer "The Adventure Shop" and is an exercise in AI assisted software development. The objective is to leverage various AI tools throughout every phase of the sofware development lifecycle to build retail systems. The intent is not "vibe coding" until something emerges, but to build intentional software products while treating AI as another member of the team. The expected result is a deeper understanding of applied AI assisted patterns, practices, and methodolgies to create quality software.

## About The Adventure Shop

The Adventure Shop (aka "the shop") is a fictional bicycle shop with ambitious growth aspirations to expand the business to more adventure sports. The shop is a small bicycle retailer who's primary focus is on bike sales and service, their customers are typically local, and range from casual riders to competitive athletes. The Adventure Shop sells products from several vendors, including various brands, bicycle types, and sizes, bike related tools, equipment, clothing, and accessories. Special order bicycles are also a core part of the business and the shop is looking to expand to build custom bikes for customers. The shop's service department is another important aspect of the business, this includes building new bikes, repairs, and routine maintenance. A smaller part of the business includes bicycle rentals, which the shop has been experimenting with more flexible rental options to offer customers.
